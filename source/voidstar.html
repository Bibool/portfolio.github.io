Creating my childhood dream.

Voidstar is my personal single-player and multiplayer spaceship game, built in Unreal Engine 5.7 with C++.

It started off as me learning the Network Prediction Plugin and ended up as a fully fledged game, inspired by my childhood favorite game: Freelancer.

The systems behind the game.

↗

Client-predicted movement

Third-person space combat with movement and rotation across all axes. Custom physics sub-stepping supports high-velocity flight.
